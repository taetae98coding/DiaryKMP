# Diary

Diary is a personal organizer that keeps your memos, tags, calendar, places, web links, contacts and songs in one place. Use it as a guest with everything stored on your device, or sign in with Google or Apple to sync across your devices.

Diary는 메모, 태그, 캘린더, 장소, 웹 링크, 연락처, 곡을 한곳에서 관리하는 개인 비서 앱입니다.

## Platforms

- Android
- iOS
- macOS
- Web
